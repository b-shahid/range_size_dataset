The file "trait_genus_github.txt" contains the information used for conducting the meta analysis for the paper titled "Plant traits influence evolutionary associations between range sizes and sexual systems in angiosperms"
The file contains information at the genus level for 37 genera:

the self-compatibility variable distinguishes between the genera including only incompatible species among the cosexual species (‘incomp’) or the presence of self-compatibility in at least some cosexual species (‘comp’); 
the fruit type variable describes whether all species within a genus (dioecious and cosexual) produce dry fruits (‘dry’), or whether at least some species display fleshy fruits (‘fleshy’); 
the pollination syndrome variable distinguishes between genera including only biotic pollination (‘biotic’) or with at least some species able of abiotic pollination (‘abiotic’); 
the woodiness variable describes whether all species in a genus are herbaceous (‘herb’), or whether at least some species are woody (‘woody’); 
the life form variable distinguishes between genera with only perennial species (‘perennial’) or with a combination of perennial species and annual ones (‘annual’). 

mean_age_dioecyR: We estimated the mean relative age of dioecy emergence in each genus across the estimated transitions between sexual systems. This was calculated as the average time of dioecy emergence across 1,000 simulations of ancestral state reconstruction using relative time scales (between 0 and 1). 
mean_age_dioecyA: The absolute age of dioecy emergence for each genus was then determined by multiplying the relative age of emergence by the genus crown age, as retrieved from the TimeTree database
